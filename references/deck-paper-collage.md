# Deck paper collage: asset prompts and acceptance

The runtime draws seeded, editable paper layers for measured list containers
and selected sparse case pages. Plan one large reading face, then one or two
smaller slips. The face carries real text, and exposed backs can carry dots or
ruled texture. Avoid evenly sized cards with interchangeable decoration.

Study a hand-torn edge in two scales. Its overall silhouette bends in a few
broad waves and its exposed fiber has smaller irregular breaks. Contrast one
torn edge with a quieter knife-cut edge. Stack large, medium and small paper
pieces. Let only their margins overlap so the eye still follows the source
order. Keep the shadow tight where the sheets touch and slightly wider at a
lifted edge. Use a fixed light direction and small, discrete offsets to borrow
the feel of frame-by-frame paper animation. Let a dot screen fade across an
exposed strip. Leave the reading face clear.

**A. Reading sheet.** Top-down scan of a broad warm cotton paper sheet with a smaller spot-ink backing peeking out under two edges. A clean cut contrasts with a hand-torn lower edge, occasional fine fibers and subtle grain. Keep the top sheet's entire center plain enough for editable body text. Place a small patch of separated halftone dots on the exposed backing. Use a tight contact shadow, slightly wider at one lifted corner. Transparent perimeter. No lettering, glossy surfaces or thick board.

**B. Controlled stop-motion variation.** Two matching top-down frames of the same three-paper swatch on an animation table. Keep camera, light and grain direction fixed. Between frames, move one strip by a tiny step and rotate it slightly. The physical change is discrete, with no motion blur. Preserve imperfect tear fibers, slightly uneven exposed edges and a short consistent shadow. One modest halftone patch clipped to an edge. Study the rhythm; keep reading text still and omit motion blur from slides.

**C. Dark-paper calibration.** Reuse A with [paper tone], [secondary spot ink] and [muted warm accent] replaced with deck palette colors. Produce one proof on warm stock and one on reversed stock. If the reversed result reads as a bright floating sticker, lower highlight contrast and crop the excess. Remove pseudo-text.

**D. Case cutaway.** Top-down macro scan of three small scraps arranged at the
edge of a reporting notebook. One inked shape from the subject sits on a matte
off-white cutout. Place a short warm backing below it and one two-color
halftone fade on an exposed edge. A left-hand torn fiber ridge meets a clean
right edge. Tight contact shadow along the base, slightly lifted tip. No words,
readable numbers, photo subjects, heavy drop shadow, or full-slide background.

Use prompts as studies of the construction. First implement the shapes with
the seeded contour, offset fiber, contact shadow and clipped dot screen. If a
specific brief needs physical fibers the native proof cannot convey, prepare
one original transparent edge or paper asset in a fixed light direction and
the existing palette. Place it only within the requested local treatment.
Keep all slide copy and chart data native and editable.

Accept the result when the assertion reads first at thumbnail size, the
headline and points retain clear insets, and the asymmetric edges and backing
guide attention without introducing a competing subject. Compare with the
v4.5 reference at the same DPI. This treatment is local to list and sparse case
pages. Keep the approved cover, divider, paper backgrounds, palette pairings
and existing ornament planning at their v4.5 appearance.
